Simulink / Simscape · personal project

Three-phase VSI: 180° conduction vs SPWM

Same RL load, two switching philosophies. The harmonic spectrum shows the trade-off: 180° keeps 5th/7th harmonics; SPWM pushes them up to the carrier band where the load inductance filters them.

SPWM
