04 · DC-bus capacitor optimisation by AC analysis

The smallest capacitor that meets the limit

Ripple vs Clink · worst case over 3 operating points
This is not “choose the largest capacitor”. A real design also depends on the network impedance, switching behaviour, operating point, ESR/ESL, the capacitor's RMS-current and thermal rating, transient (load-dump, precharge, short-circuit) requirements, and packaging. A larger C lowers ripple but adds volume, cost and stored energy (½CV²) that the discharge circuit must remove.
