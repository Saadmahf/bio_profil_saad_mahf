03 · Takagi–Sugeno representation

Four weighted local models

The products x₁x₂ and x₄x₅ are written A(z)·x with premise variables z₁ = x₂, z₂ = x₅. Two membership functions per premise give 4 local models whose weighted sum is exact inside the premise domain.
