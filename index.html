Who I am

I'm an electrical and embedded systems engineer at Capgemini Engineering, working for Stellantis in the High-Voltage Safety Integration team. My work sits where the electrical architecture of an electric vehicle meets its measurements: I validate HV networks, analyse DC-bus behaviour in time and frequency, and use simulation to explain what the signals show.

Before that I built test tools at NGE Automotive (C firmware, CAN / CAN-FD and LIN on an RP2040, PCBs, a PWM-controlled buck converter) and modelled battery management systems in MATLAB, Simulink and Stateflow. What I enjoy most is opening a complex system and understanding what is happening inside it, layer by layer.
